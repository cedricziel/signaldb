feat(ui): add the Catalog Map view for services

Adds a List | Map switch (kept in the URL as `?cview=map`) to the Catalog's
service entity type. The Map draws the tenant's whole service graph via the
`graph` envelope: RED figures per node, edge width by call rate, error
colouring at the 0.5%/2% thresholds, external dependencies shown distinct
with a hide-external toggle, and a side panel on node click with links to
the service page, its traces, and its errors. Node-cap and correlate
row-limit warnings surface above the map.

Extracts the `graph`-to-`ServiceGraph` mapping and neighbour-row helpers
(previously private to ServiceNeighborhood) into features/catalog/graphView.ts
so both maps share one definition.

Implements task 3.5 of the service-map openspec change; ticks 3.4/3.5 in
tasks.md and documents both views in docs/users/explore-ui.md.
